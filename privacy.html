Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Local Grow ("I", "me") handles information when you visit this website or get in touch about a project.

Information I Collect

When you use the contact form on this site, I collect the details you choose to enter — your name, email, phone number (optional), the type of website you're enquiring about, and your message. This site's contact form does not connect to a backend or mailing service; the information stays in your browser and is not automatically transmitted anywhere unless you also reach out via WhatsApp or Gmail.

If you contact me via WhatsApp or Gmail, I receive whatever information is included in that message, subject to WhatsApp's and Google's own privacy policies.

How Information Is Used

  • To respond to your enquiry and discuss your project
  • To prepare a quote and, if you go ahead, to deliver the agreed website
  • To keep basic records of ongoing and past projects

Your information is not sold or shared with third parties for marketing purposes.

Cookies & Local Storage

This site uses your browser's local storage only to remember your light/dark theme preference. No tracking cookies or analytics scripts are used.

Your Choices

You can ask to have any information you've shared with me deleted at any time by messaging me on WhatsApp or Gmail — contact details are on the Contact page.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated occasionally to reflect changes to the site. The "Last updated" date above will always reflect the latest version.
